VeroApp SMS Guard Privacy & Compliance Guarantee

Package / Application ID: com.veroapp.smsguard | Version: 1.0.0 | Effective Date: November 2, 2026
Fundamental Principle: Absolute On-Device Processing & Zero Data Collection
VeroApp SMS Guard does not upload, collect, or transmit your text messages, call records, contacts, location, or biometric data to external servers or third parties. All filtering and classification engines execute strictly on your device's local processor.

1. Data Processed Locally On Your Device

VeroApp SMS Guard processes the following data exclusively in local device volatile memory (RAM) or local SQLite storage to provide its core protection:

  • SMS Messages & Metadata: Incoming SMS bodies, sender addresses, and timestamps are transiently analyzed to identify phishing, illegal gambling spam, extortion threats, and OTP theft, routing them to the on-device Spam / Quarantine inbox.
  • Incoming Telephone Numbers: Inspected against the bundled offline database (spam_numbers.bin) via the official Android CallScreeningService API in < 0.1 ms to reject nuisance and fraud calls.
  • Contacts (READ_CONTACTS): Queried strictly on-device to whitelist known acquaintances with a green badge. Contacts are never duplicated, transmitted, or backed up remotely.
  • Local Quarantine Logs: Stored strictly in local SQLite and SharedPreferences; zero external telemetry or analytics upload.

2. Data We NEVER Collect or Transmit

  • We DO NOT record, listen to, or process audio conversations or microphone data.
  • We DO NOT upload, store, or forward SMS text contents to cloud servers.
  • We DO NOT collect GPS location, Advertising IDs (AAID/IDFA), or device fingerprints.
  • We DO NOT bundle third-party analytics (Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics) or advertising SDKs.

3. Declared Permissions & Store Compliance Justification

  • android.permission.RECEIVE_SMS & android.permission.READ_SMS: Required to perform real-time on-device spam detection and default SMS organization.
  • android.telecom.CallScreeningService (ROLE_CALL_SCREENING): Utilized to evaluate and block known scam numbers without invasive access to permanent call history.
  • android.permission.INTERNET: Utilized strictly for optional, user-initiated one-way binary threat signature updates from public GitHub releases; no user telemetry or identifiers are transmitted.

4. User Rights & Data Deletion Procedure

Because VeroApp SMS Guard operates with a Zero Cloud Footprint, no user data resides on remote servers. Users maintain full control over local data:

  1. In-App Log Clearance: Navigate to Settings > Clear Scan History or Reset Database to immediately wipe local logs.
  2. Operating System Purge: Clear local storage via Android/iOS Settings > Apps > VeroApp SMS Guard > Clear Storage.
  3. Application Uninstallation: Uninstalling the application permanently deletes all local SQLite databases, Bloom filters, and preference files from the device file system.
  4. Server-Side Requests: Since 0 bytes of user data exist on our infrastructure, no remote account deletion ticket is required.

5. Children's Online Privacy Protection (COPPA Compliance)

VeroApp SMS Guard is not directed to children under 13 years of age. The application does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children.

6. GDPR (EU) Articles 13 & 14 Disclosures

Data Controller: VeroApp Security Engineering Team.
Legal Basis (GDPR Art. 6): User explicit consent (Art. 6(1)(a)) and legitimate interest in providing robust cyber-defense against extortion and financial fraud (Art. 6(1)(f)).
User Rights: Under GDPR Chapter III, you have the right to access, rectify, restrict, and erase local data, as well as lodge a complaint with your national Data Protection Authority (DPA).
